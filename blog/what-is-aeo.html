What Is AEO and How Do You Get Cited by AI?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) means writing and structuring your content so AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude and Google AI Overviews can find it and quote it as an answer.

Why does AEO matter?

Many people now get answers directly from AI summaries instead of clicking ten links. If your page gives a clear answer, it has a better chance of being the source. Ranking on page one is still important, but being the quoted answer builds trust and brings visitors who already know your name.

How do you do AEO in 4 steps?

  1. Answer first. Put a one or two sentence direct answer right under each heading.
  2. Use question headings. Write H2s the way people ask, for example "What is AEO?".
  3. Add schema. Use Article and FAQ JSON-LD, as this page does.
  4. Show trust. Add author name, date, and links to reliable sources (E-E-A-T).

What does a good AEO answer look like?

Here is a simple before and after for the question "What is a meta description?".

Before: "Meta descriptions have been around for many years and there is a lot to say about them. Let us start with a bit of history..."

After: "A meta description is a short summary (about 150 characters) of a web page that appears under the title in search results. It does not directly change rankings, but a good one can improve clicks."

The second version answers the question in the first sentence. That is what AI tools look for.

What are common AEO mistakes?

  • Hiding the answer after a long introduction.
  • Using vague headings like "Overview" instead of real questions.
  • Adding FAQ schema that does not match the text visible on the page.
  • Publishing with no author, no date and no sources.

Frequently asked questions

What does AEO stand for?

Answer Engine Optimization: preparing content so AI tools and search summaries can find, trust and quote it.

Is AEO different from SEO?

AEO builds on SEO. SEO helps pages rank; AEO makes the answers inside those pages easy for AI to extract and cite.

What is the difference between AEO and GEO?

AEO focuses on giving short, direct answers to questions. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is a wider idea: making your brand and content easy for generative AI tools to understand and mention in their answers.

Does schema markup guarantee AI citations?

No. Schema helps machines understand your page, but nothing guarantees a citation. Clear answers, real expertise and trustworthy content matter most.

How long should an AEO answer be?

Keep the direct answer to one or two sentences (about 40 to 60 words), then explain the details below it.
