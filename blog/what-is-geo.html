What Is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) means improving your content and brand so generative AI tools, like ChatGPT, Claude and Google AI Overviews, can understand it and mention it when they write answers.

Why does GEO matter?

Generative AI tools do not just list links. They read many sources, then write one combined answer. If your brand and content are clear and trusted, you have a better chance of being one of the sources they use or name. If they are not, you can be left out even when you rank well in classic search.

How is GEO different from SEO and AEO?

SEO helps your pages rank in search results. AEO shapes individual answers so they are easy to quote. GEO is the wider view: how your whole brand, topics and expertise show up across AI-written answers. Think of it as three layers that work together, not three separate jobs. You can read the basics of the second layer in What Is AEO?.

How do you do GEO in 5 steps?

  1. Fix the basics first. Make sure Google and Bing can crawl and index your pages. AI tools cannot use what search engines cannot find.
  2. Write clear, complete answers. Cover a topic fully, with a short direct answer first and details below.
  3. Be specific and original. Add real examples, numbers you can source, and your own experience. Generic text is easy for AI to replace.
  4. Build your entity. Use the same brand and author name everywhere, and add Person or Organization schema so machines know who you are.
  5. Earn mentions elsewhere. Get listed and talked about on trusted sites, profiles and communities in your field.

What are common GEO mistakes?

  • Thinking GEO replaces SEO instead of building on it.
  • Filling pages with keywords instead of useful, original information.
  • Using different brand or author names on different platforms.
  • Promising clients a guaranteed mention in AI answers.

Where can you learn more?

Google explains what it looks for in quality content in its helpful content guidance, and how AI features use your pages in Search Central's guide to AI features.

Frequently asked questions

What does GEO stand for?

GEO stands for Generative Engine Optimization: improving your content and brand presence so generative AI tools can understand it and mention it in their answers.

Is GEO replacing SEO?

No. GEO builds on SEO. Pages that are crawlable, helpful and trustworthy are still the base that AI tools draw from.

How is GEO different from AEO?

AEO focuses on short, direct answers to specific questions. GEO is broader and covers how your whole brand, topics and expertise appear across generative AI answers.

Can anyone guarantee a mention in ChatGPT or AI Overviews?

No. AI answers change often and no one controls them. GEO improves your chances by making your content clear, credible and easy to reuse.

How do I start with GEO?

Start by making sure your site can be crawled and indexed, then write clear answers, add author details and schema, and build your brand mentions on trusted websites.
