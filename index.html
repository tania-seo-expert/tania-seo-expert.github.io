Frequently asked questions

Direct answers to the search questions that matter most today.

What is the role of CTR in modern organic search rankings?

Click-through rate shows how often searchers choose your result. A compelling title, meta description and rich snippet earn more clicks from the same position, and strong engagement signals help confirm that your page satisfies the query.

How does Tania Ch target a global audience for website rankings?

Tania Ch combines international keyword research, hreflang and clean site architecture, localized content, and authoritative backlinks from multiple regions, then tracks performance by country in Google Search Console and GA4.

Why is Technical SEO crucial for international indexing?

Search engines can only rank what they can crawl, render and index. Correct robots rules, sitemaps, canonical tags, hreflang, fast page speed and structured data make sure every market version of a page is discovered and understood.
